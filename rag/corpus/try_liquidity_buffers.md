# Treasury brief — liquidity buffers and roll risk

Liquidity coverage relies on high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) and stable
funding. Short-dated deposits and FX settlements create roll risk when large
maturities cluster in the same TIME_PERIOD.

When reviewing large DEPOSIT or FX observations, check whether purpose text
implies overnight or wholesale funding concentration. Buffer adequacy narratives
should cite period, currency, and magnitude from the observation — not generic
advice.

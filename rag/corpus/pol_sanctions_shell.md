# Sanctions and opaque counterparties — supervisory guidance

Payments described as routed to sanctioned counterparties, unregistered shell
entities, or unexplained offshore intermediaries are high-priority exceptions.

Even when the numerical observation passes structural SDMX checks, semantic
purpose text can indicate elevated risk. Flag for human review when purpose
language cites sanctions, shell entities, or opaque offshore routing.

Risk rating hint: treat explicit sanctions language as HIGH; opaque shell or
offshore wording without sanctions as MEDIUM–HIGH pending documentation.

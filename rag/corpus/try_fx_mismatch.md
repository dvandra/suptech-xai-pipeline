# Treasury brief — FX mismatch and swap-line usage

Cross-currency liquidity rebalancing and swap-line drawdowns can be routine,
but unexplained offshore FX routing or remittance patterns combined with
extreme OBS_VALUE warrant review.

Treasury narratives should state the currency, whether the purpose matches
normal FX activity (spot conversion, remittance, swap-line), and whether
amounts look like outliers versus the peer distribution for FX.

# Trade mis-invoicing and cross-border value transfer

Trade mis-invoicing moves value across borders by over- or under-stating
invoice amounts. Purpose text referencing mis-invoicing, rapid round-trips, or
unexplained intermediary routing should be treated as elevated risk even when
the asset class is FX or SECURITY.

Analysts should cross-check jurisdiction pairs and whether the stated purpose
is consistent with the reported asset class.

# Treasury brief — rate-sensitive positions

Interest-rate swap fixed-leg payments and bond settlements are rate-sensitive.
Large DERIVATIVE or SECURITY flows around policy-meeting months can be normal;
purpose language outside that vocabulary (e.g. layering, shell entities) is not.

When drafting treasury commentary, separate market-normal rate activity from
financial-crime typology language. Only the latter should drive HIGH risk
ratings in this simulation.

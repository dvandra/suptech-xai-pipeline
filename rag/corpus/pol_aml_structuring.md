# Supervisory typology note — cash structuring and layering

Structuring is the deliberate splitting of cash or transfers to stay below
reporting thresholds. Layering is the rapid movement of funds across accounts
or jurisdictions to obscure origin.

Supervisors should escalate when free-text purpose fields mention structuring,
threshold engineering, layering, or round-trip wires — especially combined with
amounts above typical peer activity for the asset class.

Recommended action: request supporting documentation and, where red-flag
language is explicit, escalate to the financial-crime review unit.
